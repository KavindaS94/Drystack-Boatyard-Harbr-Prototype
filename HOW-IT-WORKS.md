# How the Yard + Dry stack prototype works

A plain, step-by-step walkthrough of everything in the prototype.

---

## When you open it

1. You land on the **Calendar**.
2. On the **left** is the menu: Calendar, Launch board, Yard tablet, Settings.
3. **Top left** shows the marina name ("Harbour Demo").
4. **Bottom left** is your user button — click it to switch between **Office** (sees prices) and **Yard** (prices hidden).

---

## The Calendar (the main screen)

5. It's a grid: **boats' spaces down the side**, **days across the top**.
6. Spaces come in 3 types, shown by the filter buttons at the top right: **Berth** (boat in the water), **Dockyard** (boat lifted out for work), **Dry stack** (boat stored on a rack).
7. Click a filter to show only that type, or **All**.
8. Each coloured bar is a booking. A maintenance job's bar takes the **colour of its job type**, and if the boat is being lifted it shows the **lift time** on the bar.
9. **Click any bar** → a panel opens on the right with that boat's details.

---

## The right-side panel (when you click a boat)

10. Top shows the **boat name** and a tag saying its type (Berth / Dockyard / Dry stack).
11. Below that: the **dates, customer, boat size, and berth**.
12. What appears next depends on the type of space:

### If it's a Berth (in the water)

13. If there's no work yet, you see two buttons: **Add afloat job** and **Send to Dockyard**.
14. **Add afloat job** = do work while the boat stays in the water (engine, electrics, etc.). A job section appears with a checklist, hours, and materials.
15. **Send to Dockyard** = lift the boat out for work. This opens a small pop-up (step 20).

### If it's a Dockyard (lifted out)

16. You see the full **Job**: job type, lift time, a checklist to tick, and lines for **hours** and **materials**.
17. If the job needs signed **T&Cs**, you can't mark it done until they're signed.
18. **Create draft invoice** turns the job's hours/materials into a bill, and a banner tells you which bank account it goes to.

### If it's a Dry stack (stored boat)

19. You see its **status** (Stored / Launched / Departed) and any upcoming launch/lift tasks.

---

## Sending a boat to the dockyard (the pop-up)

20. Pick the **yard spot**, the **dates**, the **job type**, and a **lift time**.
21. Choose **Keep berth** (still pay for the water spot) or **Move (free berth)** (give it up).
22. If the boat is too long for the spot, it warns you.
23. Click **Confirm** — the boat now shows on the dockyard with its job.

---

## The Yard tablet (for the yard crew)

24. Open **Yard tablet** from the menu — it looks like a phone/tablet screen.
25. **Search** for a boat, tap it, and you get the **checklist, hours, and materials** to fill in.
26. The crew sees **no prices** here — just the work.

---

## The Launch board (for stored boats)

27. Open **Launch board** from the menu.
28. Pick a **day** — it starts on a busy Saturday with about 50 jobs.
29. Jobs are split into **Launch** (put boat in water) and **Lift** (bring boat back).
30. Each row shows the time, customer, boat, and its status.
31. Tick the checklist and press **Done** — the boat's status moves **Stored → Launched → Departed** automatically.
32. **Add task** lets reception book a new launch by searching for the customer.

---

## Settings (set up the marina)

33. Open **Settings** → tabs across the top.
34. **Modules & words** — turn Boatyard / Dry stack on or off, and rename them to your own words.
35. **Berths** — set each space's type (Berth / Dockyard / Dry stack).
36. **Job types** — your list of yard jobs, each with a colour, checklist, and products.
37. **Task types** — your launch/lift tasks.
38. **Products** — every billable item, its price, and which **bank account** it goes to.
39. Turn a module off and its menu item and screens disappear.

---

## The two roles (bottom-left user button)

40. **Office** = sees prices, invoices, and bank accounts.
41. **Yard** = same work, but **prices are hidden** — for crew who shouldn't see money.

---

That's the whole app: **Calendar** to see everything, **panel** to work on one boat, **tablet** for the yard crew, **Launch board** for stored boats, and **Settings** to configure it — all looking and behaving like the real Harbr.
